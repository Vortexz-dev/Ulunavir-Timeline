# Ambiyans ve olay sesleri — kaynaklar ve lisanslar

All audio here was downloaded from Wikimedia Commons or OpenGameArt and processed for the site: cut, loop-seam crossfade, mono, loudness-normalised, some compressed or pitched; MP3. Licences were checked per file (Commons via its API, OpenGameArt on the content page).

- **CC0 / Public domain**: no attribution required; credited anyway.
- **CC BY 3.0** (one file, `amb-fire-2.mp3`): attribution REQUIRED — “Campfire sound ambience” by Glaneur de sons, CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), modified (cut/looped/normalised/compressed).

Synthesised fallbacks (wind/fire/rain/sea/forest/drone pads, the soft chime) are generated live with WebAudio in `assets/app.js`; they have no external source.
