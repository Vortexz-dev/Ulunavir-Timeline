# Harita verileri (assets/map)

- `config.json` — ölçek (`kmPerPixel`, tam çözünürlük 5112x4920 px'e göre), `walkKmPerDay`, `horseKmPerDay`, sis/bulut ayarları. `scaleIsEstimate: true` iken rota kutusunda "ölçek tahmini" yazar.
- `places.json` — tıklanabilir yer adları (şimdilik boş). Şema:
  `{ "id": "balahnur", "name": "Balahnur", "x": 2310, "y": 1480, "type": "city|town|castle|ruin|region|sea|landmark", "tag": "Balahnur", "description": "kısa açıklama" }`
  `x,y` tam çözünürlüklü harita pikseli. `tag` verilirse tıklayınca sitenin tag kartı açılır. İsteğe bağlı: `"minZoom": 0.3` (bu yakınlığın altında gizle), `"size": 1`.
- `roads.json` — yollar (şimdilik boş). Şema:
  `{ "id": "kral-yolu", "name": "Kral Yolu", "type": "road|path|sea", "points": [[x,y],[x,y],...] }`
- `tiles/{z}/{x}_{y}.webp` — 512 px karo piramidi (z=0 en küçük 639 px … z=3 tam çözünürlük). `tiles.json` düzeyleri listeler. `base.webp` hızlı ilk görüntü.
- `fogfield.png` — kıyıya uzaklık alanı (1/4 çözünürlük; 0 = kara, 255 = kıyıdan ≥512 px). Sis yoğunluğu buradan hesaplanır. `landmask.png` önizleme maskesi.
- Kaynak üretim betikleri: /workspace/mapsrc/mask.py, tiles.py

## roads_auto.json (v2)
Hand-traced from visible painted roads/paths (automatic colour detection was too noisy against beige mountains/snow).
Same schema as roads.json. Not drawn — used only as routes for the tiny animated caravans.
As soon as roads.json has entries with 2+ points, caravans use roads.json instead.

## whirlpool.webp (v2)
Circular, de-squashed crop of the painted whirlpool (centre ~4503,1380 full-res px); rotated in place by map.js.

## v3 data
- `sealanes.json` — ship lanes: contours of the coast-distance field at 110/170/250 px from land (generated, so ships never cross land). `[{len, points:[[x,y],…]}]`.
- `life.json` — hand-placed life data: `smoke` = chimney emitters `[x, y, size, tint]` (tint 1 = dark forge smoke), `settlements` = `[x, y, radius]` used for the town soundscape.
- `roads_auto.json` — now 16 traced routes (caravans spread over them by length). `roads.json` still takes over when filled.

## t121 additions

- `places.json` — generated by `frp-timeline/site-build/map_places.py` (run from `build.py`) out of
  `site-build/map/places_src.json` (names + positions from the captioned map, sourced notes) plus
  `data.js` / `tags.js`. Shape: `{places:[{id,name,rank,x,y,lx,ly,tag,sub,desc,facts,ph,phAuto,ev,links}],
  pins:[{o,x,y,type,est,pl,via}]}`. `rank` ∈ capital|city|town|landmark|district|region|sea|hidden;
  `est:1` marks an estimated pin location; inferences in texts are marked "tahmin ediliyor".
- `roadnet.json` — generated by `site-build/map/roadnet.py` (least-cost paths on the land mask that
  follow painted roads, crossing water only at bridges/ports). `{nodes:[[x,y,kind,name]],
  edges:[{a,b,t,len,p}]}`, `t`: 0 royal, 1 trade, 2 side. Caravans travel city-to-city on it.
- `config.json` `kmPerPixel` is now 0.725.
- Day/night: 120 s cycle (`ulv-map-dn`), pins toggle (`ulv-map-pins`) in localStorage.
- Images still needed for imageless places: `frp-timeline/site-build/t121_map_img_queue.json`.

## t122 / t123 additions

- `lights.bin` — night lights, generated by `frp-timeline/site-build/map/lights.py` from the painted map
  (roof/stone/wood colours + edge energy inside place/settlement zones; Southern Lands kept sparse).
  Uint16 little-endian: `[nPts, nPools]`, then `nPts × [x, y, size*20 | bright*255<<8]` (shuffled, so drawing
  the first N is a zoom LOD), then `nPools × [x, y, radius, bright*1000]`. Drawn in one additive WebGL pass;
  twinkle, staggered switch-on and lamps going off/on are computed in the shader.
- `config.json` planner speeds: `walkKmPerDay`, `horseKmPerDay`, `cartKmPerDay` (caravan), `shipKmPerDay`
  (sea legs), `airshipKmh` × `airshipHoursPerDay` (Aetherion Magnus-type airship — an estimate, no archive figure).
- Toolbar toggles kept in localStorage: `ulv-map-roads` (Yollar layer, default off), `ulv-map-wx` (seasons & weather).
- Route planner: Dijkstra over `roadnet.json`; legs without a road connection fall back to a straight sea line
  (stated in the result).
- Test hooks: `window.__ulvMapDbg = {nofx, nolife, noships, nocars, nosmoke, nonight}` switches layers off for profiling.

## t124 additions
- `searoutes.json` (built by `site-build/map/searoutes.py`): 9 harbour quays + 36 port-to-port sea routes (A* over the
  landmask, kept away from coasts, smoothed). ~62 % of ships now leave from and arrive at city harbours on these routes.
- Pin edit mode: lock button in the left toolbar → history pins and place labels become draggable (dashed outline).
  Pressing the lock again sends only the moved positions `{moves:[{id,x,y}]}` to the Katip worker `POST /pins`
  (`https://katip-pirdeviz.ulunavir.workers.dev/pins`). Ids: `pl:<placeId>` (label position lx/ly) and `pin:<o>`
  (pin x/y). The worker accepts only known ids from the live places.json, positions inside the map bounds, at most 120
  moves per request, only from the site origin/localhost, rate-limited per IP (8/min, 80/h); there is no delete. Stored in
  KV namespace `PINS` (key `pos:v1`, audit log of the last 300 moves in `pos:log`). Every map load does `GET /pins`
  (edge-cached 15 s, client memo 60 s) and applies the overrides over places.json. Failed saves stay in
  localStorage (`ulv-pin-pending`) and are re-sent on the next open.
- Baking overrides into the repo: `python3 site-build/map/pins_bake.py && python3 site-build/map_places.py`
  (writes `site-build/map/pos_overrides.json`, which map_places.py applies when it rebuilds places.json).
- Weather: up to 4 storm cells (2 over sea, 2 over land with the weather toggle) with a rotating dark cloud mass,
  3-layer angled rain, ground darkening, lightning bolts (NGL flash quads, kind 4) and rare waterspouts; none over the
  Southern Lands, which get an occasional sandstorm (`uSand`). Snow: 3 depth layers, per-column speeds, wind drift, haze.
  Rain/snow offsets are integrated in JS and wrapped (mediump-safe).
- Nature (LIFE `NAT`): deer herds (meadows picked from the base map), whales/dolphins, eagle over the peaks, a rare dragon
  near Deathwing'in İni, a very rare faint sea serpent in the fog belt, rainbows after storms.
- Pirates (LIFE `PIRATE`): chase → broadside (muzzle flashes kind 5, smoke) → victim sinks, pirate flees and fades.
- Test hooks: `__ulvMap.fx('storm'|'land'|'sand'|'pirate'|'whale'|'dolph'|'bow'|'dragon'|'eagle'|'serp', x, y)`,
  `__ulvMapDbg.nonat`.
